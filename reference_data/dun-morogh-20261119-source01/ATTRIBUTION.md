Normalized 93 Dun Morogh source records from index/21 shards and area-map mapping.
This is a partial catalog, not exact-build authority, live availability or a best
route. Narrative excerpts are untrusted source data; no importance ranking is
assigned. Chain size is a source graph property, not proof of main-story status.
Area coordinates are percentages in their own area map, not necessarily C_Map
UI-map coordinates. They provide neither meters nor travel time. No world map
scale/transform is invented. Only pickup/turn-in reference points are normalized.
